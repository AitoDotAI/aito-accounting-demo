## Aito quirks discovered

- `$patterns` on a `Text` column is not just less legible than on `String`,
  it is **wrong** where one value's tokens are a subset of another's:
  `vendor = "Kauko Oy"` is 245 rows, the mined
  `$has "kauko" AND $has "oy"` is 331 — absorbing `Kauko Group Oy`'s 86.
  Rule support inflates 35% with a 200.
- The engine uses a linked table's column as a feature **only when it is
  tokenized**. With `vendor` as `String` the settlement-entity association
  is exactly zero: `$p` identical to five decimals whether the bank line
  names the vendor's own settler or an unrelated one.

## Surprising

- **An end-to-end accuracy run hid a completely dead feature.** The
  settlement split reported 23/23 while the model contributed nothing —
  the matcher blends `$p` with a client-side amount score, and the amount
  was doing all of it. Only a control holding the amount fixed and varying
  one field exposed it. Accuracy numbers on a blended score cannot tell a
  working model from a broken one.
