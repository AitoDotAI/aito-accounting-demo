## Aito quirks discovered

- **`$why` reconciles exactly; our extraction did not.** Verified on a
  live match: full tree product `0.905923` against `$p` `0.905923`, ratio
  `1.0000000000`, eight lift factors. We kept five and printed the
  product as an equation. Four filters prune — near-1.0 normalizers,
  near-1.0 lifts, customer-scope propositions, top-5 truncation.
- **A `_relate` response on v2 collections carries no `$has` at all.**
  Measured: `{"vendor": "Europress Group Oy"}`, zero occurrences in the
  raw response. Every `$has` our code reads is manufactured by
  `AitoV2Client.relate`. The cheatsheet had been documenting our own
  shim's output as Aito's response shape.
- Polling cost is about the table, not the query: a keyed lookup in
  `precompute_entries` (285 rows of `Text` blobs) is 0.8–5.2 s; reading
  all of `cache_versions` is 0.21 s.

## Surprising

- **`confidence` is not `$p`.** Even a perfectly reconciling chain ends
  at a different number from the headline, because confidence is
  `0.5 × model_p + 0.5 × amount_agreement`. Two correct numbers on screen
  with nothing connecting them reads exactly like a bug.
- **Importing `src.app` constructs live Aito clients and PUTs schemas at
  module level.** A `TestClient(app)` test made an unrelated formfill test
  assert `4 == 6`, intermittently, by disturbing its httpx mocking. Cost
  two wrong theories before probing it directly. Tests now import
  `src.admin_ops` instead; fixing the import-time side effects is still
  open.
- A peer session's ticket hand-off was for another lane
  (`aito-ecommerce-demo`, field `pet_type`). Declining was right, and the
  peer retracted it. Checking the same blind-spot *class* here was the
  useful half.
